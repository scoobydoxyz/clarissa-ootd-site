Privacy Policy — Clarissa ootd

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

1. Data we collect

When you connect your TikTok account through Login Kit, we receive your TikTok user ID, username and profile information as permitted by the scopes you approve. When you create a video, we process the photo and prompt you provide, and the resulting video is uploaded to your TikTok drafts through the Content Posting API.

2. How the data is used

Your TikTok account information is used only to connect your account and to upload videos to your own drafts. Your photos and prompts are used only to create the videos you request. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties except TikTok, as needed to provide the service.

3. Storage

TikTok access tokens are stored encrypted. Photos and videos you provide are kept only as long as needed to process your request, and drafts are stored by TikTok in your account.

4. Your choices

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, which revokes the app’s access. You can also delete the photos and videos you provided.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through the TikTok developer portal.
